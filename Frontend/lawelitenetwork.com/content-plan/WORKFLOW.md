# LEN content workflow (human-vetted, original)

Rule: nothing publishes without a named human reviewer. Real facts only. No invented quotes, stats, or bios.

## Stages
1. **Pick** a row from `content-plan.csv` (status: planned -> researching).
2. **Research notes**: list every claim with its source link and date. Primary sources first.
3. **Write** from the notes, in the author's own words. Do not paraphrase a single source paragraph by paragraph. Vary structure between pieces.
4. **Fact-check**: a second person confirms each claim against its source. Mark `fact_check_done`.
5. **Similarity check**: run the text through a plagiarism checker. Record the score. Over a few percent outside quotes = rewrite.
6. **Legal/sensitive check**: anything about a living person's alleged wrongdoing gets attorney/editor review; attribute allegations, don't assert them.
7. **Images**: licensed or public-domain only, with credit.
8. **Sign-off**: reviewer fills `human_reviewer` and `signed_off_date`. Only then add to CMS.
9. **Corrections**: log any fix on the /corrections page.

## Never
- Publish a stat or quote you cannot link to a source.
- Reuse a competitor's structure or sentences.
- Use AI output as the final text. AI may help with outlines and source lists only.

## Order to write
Priority 1 rows first (entertainment hubs, top people, first three cases), then 2, then 3.
