## CMS RECONCILIATION IMPLEMENTATION (2026-09-21, local; the CMS was only read with GET requests, production was not contacted)

### Category resolution fix

With several top-level categories and no matching `categoryId` it returns nothing rather than guessing (ambiguity documented; not observed in the 10 records).
